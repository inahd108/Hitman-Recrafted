# Recrafted Arsenal — TACZ gun pack

Original weapons for the Recrafted: Contracts mission, built for
[Timeless and Classics Zero](https://modrinth.com/mod/timeless-and-classics-zero) **1.2.0** on Forge 1.20.1.

| Weapon | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Argento .45** | Pistol | Long-slide stainless .45, scroll engraving, ivory grips with gold medallions. 8 rounds, semi-auto, strong headshot multiplier. Uses TACZ `.45 ACP` ammo. |
| **Argento Suppressor** | Muzzle attachment | Fits the Argento and TACZ's pistol muzzle devices. Cuts the shot's audible range by 30 blocks. |
| **Passero .22** | Pistol | Compact satin-black .22 with an integral suppressor, walnut grips. 10 rounds of TACZ `.22 WMR`; low damage, 3x headshots, heard from only a few blocks. |
| **Bastone 9** | Pistol | The Corvara police sidearm: parkerized slide, polymer frame, rail, night sights. 15 rounds of `9mm`, loud. Takes pistol muzzle devices, extended mags and a pistol laser. |
| **Lupara** | Shotgun | Sawn-off side-by-side 12 gauge: case-hardened action with scroll engraving, external hammers, double triggers, walnut stock sawn off behind the wrist. Two shells of TACZ `12 gauge`; fire one barrel, or switch fire mode to let both go at once. Breaks open to reload. Very loud. |
| **Halcyon PDW** | SMG | Halcyon lab security's compact PDW: off-white shroud with a teal band, graphite lower, folding foregrip, ring sights, collapsed stock. 30 rounds of TACZ `4.6x30`, auto or semi. Takes muzzle devices (suppressors too) and extended magazines (36/42/50). |
| **Bulwark AR** | Rifle | Full-size 5.56 rifle: black anodized receivers, octagonal vented handguard with a full-length rail, folding sights, bronze ported brake and bolt carrier, olive-drab grip, curved magazine and stock. 30 rounds of TACZ `5.56x45`, auto or semi. Takes muzzle devices and extended magazines (40/50/60). |
| **Vento .357** | Revolver | Deep-blued full-lug revolver with a nickel fluted cylinder and rosewood grips. Six rounds of `.357 Magnum`, a short double-action trigger delay, heavy recoil and very loud. Reloads by swinging out the cylinder, punching out the empties and feeding a speed loader. |

More weapons are on the way (see the main README's roadmap).

**In the mission:** with the Recrafted mod, guards carry these guns (Lupara, slung: estate guards; Vento:
bodyguards; Halcyon PDW, slung: lab guards; Bulwark AR, slung: Halcyon security; Bastone: harbour police;
Argento: Capitano Sordi) and drop them, with
a spare reload, when knocked out or killed. In a fight they draw and shoot. Press **X** to throw a gun. See the main README for details.

## Install

1. Install TACZ 1.2.0 for Forge 1.20.1.
2. Put `recrafted_arsenal.zip` (from `dist/` on this branch) into the `tacz` folder inside your game directory
   (`.minecraft/tacz/`, created the first time TACZ runs). Don't unzip it.
3. Restart the game, or run `/tacz reload`.

The guns appear in TACZ's creative tab and the Gun Smith Table.
To spawn one directly:

```
/give @p tacz:modern_kinetic_gun{GunId:"recrafted:argento",GunCurrentAmmoCount:8,HasBulletInBarrel:1b,GunFireMode:"SEMI"}
/give @p tacz:attachment{AttachmentId:"recrafted:argento_suppressor"}
/give @p tacz:ammo{AmmoId:"tacz:45acp"} 64
/give @p tacz:modern_kinetic_gun{GunId:"recrafted:passero",GunCurrentAmmoCount:10,HasBulletInBarrel:1b,GunFireMode:"SEMI"}
/give @p tacz:modern_kinetic_gun{GunId:"recrafted:bastone",GunCurrentAmmoCount:15,HasBulletInBarrel:1b,GunFireMode:"SEMI"}
/give @p tacz:modern_kinetic_gun{GunId:"recrafted:vento",GunCurrentAmmoCount:6,GunFireMode:"SEMI"}
/give @p tacz:ammo{AmmoId:"tacz:357mag"} 36
/give @p tacz:modern_kinetic_gun{GunId:"recrafted:lupara",GunCurrentAmmoCount:2,GunFireMode:"SEMI"}
/give @p tacz:ammo{AmmoId:"tacz:12g"} 24
/give @p tacz:modern_kinetic_gun{GunId:"recrafted:halcyon",GunCurrentAmmoCount:30,HasBulletInBarrel:1b,GunFireMode:"AUTO"}
/give @p tacz:ammo{AmmoId:"tacz:46x30"} 120
/give @p tacz:modern_kinetic_gun{GunId:"recrafted:bulwark",GunCurrentAmmoCount:30,HasBulletInBarrel:1b,GunFireMode:"AUTO"}
/give @p tacz:ammo{AmmoId:"tacz:556x45"} 120
```

## How it is made

Everything is generated by `tools/armory/build.py` — no hand-edited binary files:

- `tools/armory/geomodel.py` — builds the Bedrock geometry TACZ reads, packs and paints the texture,
  and writes a Blockbench project for each model.
- `tools/armory/materials.py` — procedural stainless, bluing, ivory, gold, checkering, engraving and lettering.
- `tools/armory/guns/<gun>.py` — each gun part by part (`argento`, `passero`, `bastone`, `vento`, `lupara`, `halcyon`, `bulwark`).
- `tools/armory/guns/common.py` — the bone rig, hands and camera anchors all pistols share.
- `tools/armory/guns/pistol_anim.py` — draw, holster, fire, tactical and empty reloads, inspect; each
  gun tunes it in its `<gun>_anim.py` (slide travel, recoil, pace, sounds).
- `tools/armory/guns/revolver_anim.py` — the revolver set: double-action trigger (`charge_in`/`charge_out`,
  played by TACZ's revolver state machine), swing-out cylinder, ejector, speed loader, cylinder-spin inspect.
- `tools/armory/guns/smg_anim.py` — magazine-fed automatic set (PDW and rifle): bursts with a cycling bolt, the
  left hand coming off the foregrip or handguard to swap the magazine, then the charging handle (PDW) or the bolt
  release (rifle) on an empty gun, inspect with a magazine check.
- `tools/armory/guns/breakaction_anim.py` — the double-barrel set: one barrel or both, break open, extract and
  reload one or two shells, inspect with a look into the chambers. Timed to TACZ's double-barrel recordings, and
  uses TACZ's double-barrel fire logic and state machine.

First-person hands are your own player arms: TACZ draws them at the model's `lefthand_pos` and
`righthand_pos` bones, which the animations move.

**Blockbench:** open `gunpack/blockbench/*.bbmodel` to view or hand-polish any model; it carries its
texture inside. Export it back as *Bedrock geometry* to `assets/recrafted/geo_models/...` to use your edit.

Sounds borrow TACZ's bundled pistol, revolver, shotgun, PDW and rifle sounds, so the pack needs TACZ's default pack present
(it always is unless you delete it).
